Privacy & data

What we store
Your reading history (books, ratings, dates, notes), your email address, and your reflection journal entries. Nothing else.

Where it's stored
All data is stored in Supabase, a secure cloud database. Each user's data is isolated — no one else can see yours.

What we don't do
We do not sell your data, share it with third parties, use it for advertising, or analyse it for any purpose other than showing it back to you.

AI features
When you use AI recommendations, your reading history is sent to Anthropic's API to generate responses. Your API key is stored locally in your browser only — it never touches our servers.

Your rights
You can export your data at any time using the Download library button above. You can delete your account and all associated data by contacting us.

Third-party services
Book metadata is sourced from Open Library (Internet Archive) and Google Books, used under their respective terms of service.
